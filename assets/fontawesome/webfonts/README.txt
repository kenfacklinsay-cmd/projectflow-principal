Place here the Font Awesome webfont files so icons work locally.

From your Font Awesome 6 package, copy into this folder:
  - fa-solid-900.woff2
  - fa-solid-900.ttf
  - fa-regular-400.woff2
  - fa-regular-400.ttf
  - fa-brands-400.woff2
  - fa-brands-400.ttf

You can get them from: https://fontawesome.com/download (Free for Web)
Then open the zip and copy the contents of the "webfonts" folder here.
